Common questions


    

      
My sign-in code never arrived

      
There are no passwords in homeroom. You put in your email, we send a code. If it hasn't turned up after a minute or two:

      
    
        
  1. Look in spam. The first one often lands there, and once you move it over the rest behave.
    2. 
        
  2. Check you're using the address you joined with. A code sent to an address with no account looks exactly the same from your end.
    3. 
        
  3. Ask for a fresh code. The old one dies the moment a new one goes out, so use the newest email, not the first.
    4. 
      

      
Still nothing? Tell us above and we'll go and look.

    


    

      
How do I get everyone else in?

      
Settings, then Invite. You get a link you can text or email. Whoever opens it joins and picks their name. No email address, no password, nothing for them to set up, which is how a seven-year-old ends up checking off their own chores.

      
Think of it as a key to your calendar. Anyone who has it can walk in, so send it to people directly rather than putting it in a group chat that outlives the group.

    


    

      
Can I see our week in Apple Calendar?

      
Yes. Settings, then Calendar feed, gives you a link to subscribe to in Apple Calendar, Google Calendar, or anything else that takes a calendar URL.

      
It runs one way. What you put in homeroom shows up over there. What you add over there stays there, it won't come back to us.

    


    

      
If I mark something private, who sees it?

      
You, and nobody else. Private items don't show to the rest of the household, don't appear in anything you share, and stay off the kitchen display. That's the place for the surprise party, the appointment, the thing you'd rather a babysitter didn't read off the fridge screen.

    


    

      
How do I change or cancel our plan?

      
Billing lives on the web rather than in the app. Open homeroom in a browser, go to Settings, and follow the billing link. Change it or cancel whenever you like. If you cancel you keep everything until the end of what you've already paid for.

    


    

      
How do I delete our household and everything in it?

      
Settings, then Delete my household. We'll ask you to type DELETE first, because there's no getting it back.

      
It takes the household, everything inside it, every sign-in attached to it, and the record our payment processor holds. If you're in someone else's household and you want your own access gone, write to us and we'll take care of it the same day we see it.

      
Remove person is the gentler one. It takes a single member out and leaves the household where it was.

    


    

      
I think I found a bug

      
Please tell us. Honestly, the bug reports are some of the most useful mail we get, and a screenshot is worth more than a paragraph.

    

  


  

    
One thing we'd ask

    
homeroom is built for the running of a house. Who's where, what needs doing, what's for dinner. It isn't a medical record or a safe.

    
Invite links and share links are made to be passed around, which is the whole point of them, so keep the sensitive specifics out of titles and notes. "Appointment at 3" does the job. Anything you'd rather keep close, mark it private, and it stays out of every link and off the kitchen screen.
