You're one of our first families

Which means you're getting homeroom before it's in the App Store. It still installs like a real app — there's just no store download involved. Setup takes about ten minutes.

Before you start Use Safari on iPhone or iPad, and Chrome on Android. Other browsers can't add apps to your home screen. One adult sets up the household first — everyone else joins after.

1 For the parent setting things up

About five minutes

1

Open homeroom

Tap the button below in Safari or Chrome.

Open homeroom →
2

Sign in

Enter your email and tap Send me a sign-in link. Check your inbox and tap the link — there's no password to create or remember.

Not there in a minute? Check spam. It comes from hello@homeroomforfamily.com.

3

Set up your household

Three quick screens: name your household, add everyone who lives there (pets included — each person gets their own color), and optionally pick starter packs of chores and meals so you don't begin with an empty app.

4

Add it to your home screen ⭐

Don't skip this — it's what turns homeroom into a real app instead of a browser tab.

📱 iPhone & iPad

  1. Tap the Share button (square with an arrow)
  2. Scroll to Add to Home Screen
  3. Tap Add

🤖 Android

  1. Tap the ⋮ menu, top right
  2. Tap Install app
  3. Tap Install
5

Get the invite link for your family

In the app, tap the settings icon (top right) → Family invite link → Copy link. Text or AirDrop it to everyone in your household.

2 For everyone else

About two minutes each — kids included

1

Tap the invite link

Open it in Safari (iPhone) or Chrome (Android). You'll join the household automatically — no email, no password, nothing to sign up for.

2

Add it to your home screen

Same as above: Share → Add to Home Screen on iPhone, or ⋮ → Install app on Android.

3

Tap your name

Open the app and tap your own name at the top. That's it.

For kids: tap your name, see your tasks, tap to check them off. Confetti happens. 🎉

✨ Two things worth doing

Optional, but this is where homeroom really clicks

🖥

Put homeroom on a kitchen tablet

Any old iPad or Android tablet works. Open the invite link on it first, then go to app.homeroomforfamily.com/wall for a big, tappable display of today — the schedule, tonight's dinner, everyone's tasks.

To keep it awake: Settings → Display & Brightness → Auto-Lock → Never. To lock it to homeroom, turn on Guided Access under Accessibility.

📅

Get homeroom events in your normal calendar

Tap settings → Calendar subscriptions → Open, then Subscribe next to your name. homeroom events flow into Apple or Google Calendar automatically from then on — reminders included.

Questions we expect

Do I need to download anything from the App Store?

No. homeroom installs straight from the web. The App Store version is coming, and you won't lose anything when it does.

Is my family's information private?

Yes. Your household's data is visible only to your household. We don't show ads, don't track anyone, and never sell data. Full details at our privacy policy.

Do my kids need email addresses or passwords?

No. They join with the link you send and just tap their name.

Can I get my data out?

Any time. Settings → Your data → Download gives you everything as a spreadsheet or a full backup file.

How do I add someone later — a babysitter, or grandma visiting?

In the app: + Add → Person, then send them the invite link. When they leave, remove them and tap Reset link so the old one stops working.

Something's broken, or I have an idea.

Please tell us — that's the whole point of you being here early. Email support@homeroomforfamily.com. Screenshots help enormously.

💛 After your first week

No essay required — but if you have five minutes, these four answers are worth more to us than anything else

  1. What did you actually use every day?
  2. What did you expect to find and couldn't?
  3. Did the rest of your family use it, or was it mostly you?
  4. What made you want to close the app?
Especially that last one Be blunt. Knowing what made you put your phone down is how this gets good.
