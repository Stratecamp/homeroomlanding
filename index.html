Get the app

On your phone in about ten minutes.

One adult sets up the household first. Everyone else joins with a link.

No app store required.

homeroom installs straight from the web and lives on your home screen with its own icon, just like any other app. Use Safari on iPhone and iPad, or Chrome on Android. Other browsers can't add apps to your home screen.

Open homeroom
  1. Open homeroom

    Go to app.homeroomforfamily.com in SafariChrome.

  2. Sign in with your email

    Tap Send me a sign-in link, then tap the link in your inbox. There's no password to create or forget.

  3. Set up your household

    Name it, add everyone who lives there (pets too), and give each person a color. Starter chore lists and meal ideas are there if you want them.

  4. Add it to your home screen

    Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow), scroll down, tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.

    Tap the ⋮ menu at the top right, tap Install app or Add to Home screen, then Install.

    Open homeroom from the new icon from now on.

  5. Invite your family

    In the app, tap ⚙ then Family invite link and copy it. Text it to everyone. They open it, add homeroom to their home screen, and tap their name. For kids, that's the whole setup.

💻
Open it on your computer

Questions

Good to know.

Does it work on Android?

Yes. homeroom works the same on iPhone and Android. Install it from Chrome on Android and from Safari on iPhone. A household can mix both.

Do my kids need email addresses or phones?

No email and no password. Kids join with the family link and tap their name. Kids without a phone can use the kitchen display, and pre-readers get a printable picture chart.

Does it work for single-parent households?

Absolutely. homeroom takes the tracking out of your head so you're not the only place the plan lives. Kids see their own schedule and chores, and a co-parent, grandparent, or sitter can join to see exactly what they're covering. You decide who's in.

Can I use it on my computer at work?

Yes. Sign in at app.homeroomforfamily.com in any browser and you'll see the same household as everyone's phones. There's nothing to install.

Can grandparents or a sitter join?

Yes. Add them as a person and send the invite link. An adult in the household approves every new join. When a sitter moves on, remove them and reset the link so the old one stops working.

How does it work with my Apple or Google Calendar?

homeroom sends your events into Apple or Google Calendar through a calendar subscription, one feed per person plus one for the whole family. Changes you make in homeroom flow out to those calendars. Events you add directly in Apple or Google Calendar stay there and don't come into homeroom.

Is my family's information private?

Your household's data is only visible to your household. We don't show ads, don't track anyone, and never sell data. Read the full privacy policy.

What happens after the free trial?

How do I cancel?

From Settings in the app, any time. Your plan stays active until the end of the period you paid for.

Can I get my data out?

Any time. In the app, tap ⚙ then Your data then Download, and you'll get everything as a spreadsheet or a full backup file.

Something isn't working. Who do I talk to?

Email support@homeroomforfamily.com. Screenshots help a lot, and a real person reads every message.
